BirdieScore Privacy Policy

We store data like scores, active matches, match history, and selected settings(number of games/points per game) on device.
We do not transmit this match data off of the Apple Watch, nor do we use analytics, advertising or tracking services based on said data.
Active match progress remains until overwritten, discarded, or match is completed. Match History retains 25 most recent matches.
Users can delete match histories or discard active match data.
We do not store any personal user data aside from the ones stated above.
