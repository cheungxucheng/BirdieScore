Your quick guide to BirdieScore

01

Set up your match

  • Choose Pts and Best Of at the top of Home. Your selections apply to the next new match.
  • Tap New Match to start scoring with your selected format.
  • Have a match in progress? Tap Resume Match to pick up where you left off.
  • Open Match History to review previously saved results.
02

Keep the score

  • Tap the top red panel for your team, or the bottom blue panel for your opponents.
  • Made a mistake? Tap the gray Undo button at the bottom right to reverse the last point.
  • The header shows the current game number and previous game scores.
  • To leave a match, tap the × at the top left and confirm that you want to discard it.
03

Look back at results

  • Confirm and save a completed match to add it to Match History.
  • Tap the trash button beside a match to delete that individual result.
  • To clear your history, use the trash button at the top right and confirm your choice.
  • Check your results before deleting them. History deletion cannot be undone.

A few common
questions.

Still stuck? Send us an email. We’d like to hear what happened.

Why didn’t my Watch register a tap?

Make sure the display is fully awake before tapping a score panel. If a tap still doesn’t register, try again once the screen is active. If it happens repeatedly, contact support with your Watch model and watchOS version.

I closed the app during a match. Is my score lost?

Match progress saves automatically. Reopen BirdieScore and tap Resume Match on Home to continue your unfinished match. Undo history from before the app restarted is not restored.

I tapped the wrong score and ended the match.

Before saving the result, tap the Undo button at the top left of the match results screen. It reverses the last point and returns you to the match. Undo is unavailable after you confirm and save the result, or if the app restarted and the Undo history was lost.
